## Generation Prompt

A character reference sheet, clean manga-influenced illustration style matching a Japanese corporate knowledge-comic house style: clean smooth outlines, bright even lighting, soft warm skin tones, flat clean colors, no heavy dramatic shadows.

Eight characters arranged in a clean grid on a plain white background, each in a simple front-facing standing pose (half-to-full body), with a small Japanese name label printed beneath each character.

1. さくら（新入社員・女性）: early 20s Japanese woman, semi-long black hair, beige cardigan over white blouse, ID badge on a lanyard around her neck, slightly nervous but friendly expression, holding a small notebook.

2. はると（新入社員・男性）: early 20s Japanese man, short neat black hair, white dress shirt with a loosely tied navy necktie, navy slacks, ID badge on a lanyard around his neck, slightly nervous but friendly expression.

3. ミナ（社員・女性）: late 20s Japanese woman, black bob hairstyle, navy blazer over a light blue shirt, calm and confident expression, no glasses, no visible ID lanyard.

4. けん（社員・男性）: late 20s to early 30s Japanese man, short neat black hair, light blue dress shirt with sleeves rolled up, calm and confident expression, no visible ID lanyard.

5. 田中部長（管理職・男性）: mid-40s to 50s Japanese man, rectangular-framed glasses, black hair with gray streaks, dark navy business suit with necktie, arms crossed, authoritative composed expression.

6. 佐藤課長（管理職・女性）: 40s Japanese woman, thin slightly-rounded-framed glasses (visibly different shape from 田中部長's glasses so the two are easy to tell apart), neat shoulder-length or bobbed black hair, dark navy or charcoal pantsuit, holding a tablet, confident composed expression.

7. 生成AIくん（AIアシスタント・生成AIの擬人化）: an original, non-trademarked personified AI assistant robot — rounded white and light-gray egg-shaped head and body, large glowing light-blue round eyes as its only facial feature (no visible mouth or ears), thin blue accent line details on chest and shoulders, simple rounded hands, friendly neutral expression.

8. ロジックくん（既存処理・ロジックベースシステムの擬人化）: a boxy, angular, retro computer-terminal-like robot character, but deliberately CUTE and CHIBI-proportioned rather than heavy or rugged — roughly 2 to 2.5 heads tall, big head, small compact body, softly rounded corners on every edge (not sharp industrial edges). Matte, lightweight-looking plastic/resin casing (not metallic or heavy-machinery textured), beige-to-light-gray color. Its face is a small rounded-corner square screen showing a plain fixed dot/blank display (not expressive glowing eyes). A short, thin antenna and a small physical dial/switch on its head and chest — kept delicate and small, not bulky. Its limbs stay blocky/geometric to keep the "logic-based system" identity, but are short, stubby, and rounded at the joints rather than chunky or robust. An amber-to-green indicator light on its chest (a clearly different accent color from 生成AIくん's blue). Overall it should still visually read as "the old rule-based system" in contrast to "the new generative AI," but come across as endearing and approachable, not tough or industrial.

COLOR PALETTE:
- さくら / はると (newcomers): white, beige, light gray
- ミナ / けん (employees): navy, light blue, white
- 田中部長 / 佐藤課長 (management): dark navy, charcoal, white
- 生成AIくん (generative AI): white, light gray, accent blue (#4A90D9)
- ロジックくん (legacy rule-based system): beige, light gray casing, accent amber-green (#E8A33D / #6FCF97)

White background, no scenery, flat even studio lighting, consistent line weight and rendering across all eight characters, clear small Japanese name labels beneath each one. 生成AIくん and ロジックくん must be placed near each other and read as an obvious contrast pair (organic glowing-eyed AI vs. boxy mechanical legacy system) even at a glance. This is a reusable reference sheet — character proportions, hairstyles, and outfits must stay clean and distinct enough to be recognizable individually in future single-character close-ups.
